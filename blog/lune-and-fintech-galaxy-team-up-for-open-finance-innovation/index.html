Lune and Fintech Galaxy Team Up for Open Finance Innovation

We're excited to bring you some thrilling news - Lune and Fintech Galaxy have entered into a Memorandum of Understanding.

Introducing a Powerful Partnership


We're excited to bring you some thrilling news - Lune and Fintech Galaxy have entered into a Memorandum of Understanding, signifying the beginning of a promising strategic collaboration in the realm of Open Banking and Open Finance within the Middle East and Africa (MEA) region.

Shaping the Future of Finance


Lune's mission has always been to simplify financial data for all. We use state-of-the-art analytics to empower businesses to drive revenue and deliver personalized experiences based on actual spending and earning habits.

Similarly, Fintech Galaxy stands as an innovation catalyst in the fintech sector. Based in the UAE, they are building bridges in the global fintech ecosystem, encouraging Open Banking API integration, and charting the course towards an inclusive future for financial services.

The Vision of Our Partnership


By pooling our resources and expertise, we aim to revolutionize Open Banking and Open Finance processing solutions. Our goal is to keep customers at the heart of everything we do, crafting services that meet their unique needs and fostering an environment rich in knowledge exchange.

Looking Forward

This partnership signals the commencement of an exhilarating journey. Lune and Fintech Galaxy stand on the cusp of a transformative phase in the Open Finance sector, exploring the immense potential of our combined forces. We're excited to invite other forward-thinking organizations to join us in this exploration.
