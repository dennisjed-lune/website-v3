Retail bank · UAE

ADIB shows customers where their money goes.

Client
Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank
Lune products
Enrich, personal finance SDK
Category accuracy
98%

The challenge

ADIB wanted to give customers useful insight into their spending. Raw transaction descriptions were not clear. Too many transactions had no category.

What Lune delivered

Enrich adds the merchant name, logo, category and subcategory to each transaction. The Lune SDK gives ADIB ready personal finance screens.

The outcome

  • Customers see the real merchant behind each payment.
  • Customers track spending, saving and goals in the ADIB app.
  • ADIB can build merchant offers on the same data.
