Merchants run their campaigns in their own portal.

Your team does no campaign setup. The merchant creates the campaign, pays the cashback and sees its own results.

The merchant sets the goal, the budget and the cashback.

A merchant sets up a campaign and sends it for review. The merchant pays cashback only on purchases that meet the terms.

  • Goal and budget. A goal, a total budget, the cashback rate, a cap and a minimum spend.
  • Two budget modes. Run until the budget is spent, or run for fixed dates.
  • Each brand and store. Several brands, with merchant IDs and terminal IDs.

The merchant sees the return on each campaign.

The same portal shows the sales and the cost of each campaign. The merchant sees only its own campaigns.

  • Sales after refunds. Sales that earned cashback for each campaign.
  • Return on cashback. Sales for each unit of cashback spent.
  • Net cashback cost. Cashback accrued minus cashback reversed by refunds.

Each offer reaches the customers who match the goal.

The merchant selects a goal for the campaign. Lune builds the audience for that goal from your enriched transactions.

Goal: acquire customers

New to the brand

Customers who spend in the category but not at this brand. Example: they buy coffee each week, but not at this café.

Goal: win back customers

Stopped buying

Customers who bought from the brand before and then stopped. Example: no purchase in the last 90 days.

Goal: increase frequency

Buy now and then

Customers who buy from the brand, but not often. Example: one purchase a month or less.

Goal: grow average spend

Small baskets

Customers who buy from the brand with a low amount for each purchase. A minimum spend on the offer moves them up.

More ways to target

By category or subcategory

Customers who spend in one category, such as Dining, or one subcategory, such as Food Delivery.

More ways to target

By spend level

Customers above or below a spend amount in a period. Example: more than AED 2,000 on Travel in six months.

More ways to target

By card programme

Your team selects the card programmes that can earn. Example: Platinum credit only.

  • Audience size first. Your team sees how many customers can earn before the offer goes live.
  • Your data stays yours. Each bank's audience comes from its own customers and transactions.
  • Terms written for you. Lune writes the offer terms from the campaign settings.
  • No work for your data team. Lune builds each audience. Your team approves the offer.

Your team controls and measures each offer in LuneHub.

LuneHub is the portal for your bank. Your team approves the offers, selects the cards and sees the results.

No offer goes live without your approval.

The offer arrives in the Campaign Manager in LuneHub. Your team sees the terms, the stores and the audience.

  • One inbox. Pending, accepted, completed and rejected offers, with search.
  • Accept or reject. One decision for each offer.
  • Clear terms. Cashback rate, budget, cap, dates and refund window.

Your team sees the performance and the cashback of each offer.

Campaign Analytics in LuneHub shows all your offers in one view. The figures are for your bank only.

  • Offer performance. Activation rate, redemptions and spend at merchants for each offer.
  • Cashback. Cashback earned by your customers, net of refunds.
  • All offers in one view. Totals for the period, and each offer in one table.

Customers join once, activate offers with one tap and track their rewards.

Pulse runs in your mobile app through the Lune SDK for iOS and Android. It uses your bank's name and colour.

Screens from the customer app without a bank colour.

Card-linked, affiliate and promo-code offers in one programme.

Your customers see each offer type in the same place in your app.

Card-linked offers

Merchant-funded cashback. The customer activates the offer, then pays by card in store or online.

Affiliate offers

Cashback from online brands through affiliate networks. The customer shops through the offer link.

Promo codes

Discount codes from online brands. The customer copies the code and uses it at checkout.

Cashback or points, with tiers and missions.

You choose how your customers earn.

Cashback

Customers earn money back on each eligible purchase.

Points

Customers earn points and redeem them for cash value. You set the expiry dates.

Tiers

Customers move up tiers on the points they earn in 12 months.

Missions and badges

Bonus rewards for repeat spend, such as five purchases in a month.

Lune checks each purchase against the offer terms.

Budgets stay in limits

A campaign closes when its budget runs out. No customer earns more than the cap.

Refunds handled

A refund in the refund window reduces the cashback.

Confirmed after the window

Cashback becomes final when the refund window ends.

A full record

Each purchase has a recorded result, with the reason when it does not earn.
