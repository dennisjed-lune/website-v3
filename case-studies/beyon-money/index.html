How Beyon Money Uses Lune's API-First Infrastructure toElevate Digital Banking Engagement in Bahrain

Elevating Digital Banking

In today’s fast-paced digital world, customers expect more than just traditional banking. Beyon Money, Bahrain’s forward-thinking financial institution, sought to redefine user engagement by providing customers with a clearer financial picture. They envisioned a seamless, data-driven experience where users could track their spending, gain financial awareness, and receive personalized insights—all within a single app.To make this vision a reality, Beyon Money partnered with Lune.

How does the integration of Lune Enrich and insight transform Beyon Money's app?

Beyon Money implemented a joint combination of Lune’s Enrich and Insights solutions to entirely power their flagship in-app"Insights" feature, giving users a real-time, categorized view of their historical spending. This targeted integration delivers four clear user advantages:

Users spend more time on the Beyon Money app, boosting engagement.

Beyon Money can partner with merchants to deliver hyper-personalized offers based on user spending habits.

Customers gain better financial awareness, leading to more informed money management.

The bank can nudge users toward relevant financial products.

How did Lune overcome the data storage regulations in Bahrain?

Deploying digital updates in Bahrain frequently poses an immense structural challenge due to new Central Bank data storage and compliance regulations. Lune overcame this hurdle through its flexible tech architecture:

  • Seamless Under 3-Month Implementation: Lune’s clean, API-first approach allowed Beyon Money to go fully live in under three months, ensuring a fast, smooth, and low-cost integration.
  • Platform-Flexible Compliance: Lune’s platform-flexible technology ensured a quick operational turnaround while maintaining absolute compliance with local data storage boundaries, making it one of the few market solutions capable of easily adapting to Bahrain’s regulatory landscape.
