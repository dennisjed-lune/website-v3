What transaction visibility problem did D360 Bank need to solve?

For too long, banking transactions in the Middle East have been cluttered with generic holding company names, making it nearly impossible for customers to track where their money is actually going. D360, a pioneering new-age bank backed by Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF), set out to change that. They envisioned a banking experience that delivers transparency, personalization, and next-level financial insights—especially for the younger generation who expect a mobile-first approach from their banks. To bring this vision to life, D360 partnered with Lune.

    How did Lune give D360 a competitive edge in the KSA market?

    With Lune’s Enrich, D360 is actively redefining what digital banking means in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia by capturing three distinct benefits:

    Unmatched Transparency: Customers can instantly recognize exactly where their money is going, heavily fostering financial awareness and consumer trust.

    Next-Level Personalization: Clean, accurately categorized data allows D360 to deliver hyper-personalized banking experiences to its users.

    Tech-First Banking for a Young Audience: By deploying a mobile-first, data-driven approach, D360, is setting the benchmark, standard, for what modern banking should look like in KSA.

    How does Lune Enrich deliver clean, actionable data for D360?

    Lune’s Enrich solution transforms raw transaction data into clear, brand-specific insights—a massive shift in a region where most transactions display only family holding or group names:

    • Overcoming Global Competitors: Unlike global players who have struggled to achieve accuracy in the Middle East, Lune’s AI-driven technology delivers 98% accuracy in translating raw transaction data into clear categories.
    • Complete Visual Clarity: The integration ensures that both D360 and its customers can instantly see the exact brand name and high-quality logo for every single transaction.
